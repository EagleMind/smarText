How sums work

Write a number with a unit, like 46g, 2 cups, 45 min, $12.50 or 20%. Amounts of the same kind add up.

Units mix: 1 kg and 500 g make 1.5 kg, and 2 cups and 100 ml make 2.42 cups.

Each amount shows its share, and the total appears under the last line.

To start a new sum, add a heading or a line without an amount.

Click a total to change it. Every amount in that sum is rescaled to match.

Understood: weight, volume, length, area, time, energy, power, data, pieces, money and percentages. Cups, spoons, pints and gallons are US measures. Currencies are never exchanged, so dollars and euros add up apart.
